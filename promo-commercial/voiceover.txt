Peace can begin in a single moment — when we notice someone, and choose words that lift them up.

Mother's Love makes recognition a daily practice. See something good? Tap Share a recognition. Choose the expressions that fit the moment. Add your message. Preview it. And send it from the heart.

The nine expressions give everyday words to gratitude, understanding, forgiveness, patience, listening, encouragement, consideration, support, and praise.

Then watch that one small recognition join a living stream — on the phone, and on the large display — so kind words can be seen, shared, and practiced by more people.

Beginning of Peace: The Words of Mother's Love.
