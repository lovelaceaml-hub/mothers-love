3. Camera movement continues as a slow arc and approach during reading, followed by travel through connected locations. Full reading time, deliberate holds, owner pauses, reduced-motion cuts and arrival return remain intact.
4. A new recognition emerges inside the existing luminous portal, receives its reading interval in the environment, and joins the normal world. No new live record is fabricated to test it.
5. Personal textures and references clear immediately with the verified feed lifecycle and on disposal. Only fictional examples enter saved screenshots and tests.
6. Deploy the corrected preview, visually inspect it and verify world coordinates/projected movement, autonomous touring, text completeness and clearing. Update the guide and privately back up the verified source.
