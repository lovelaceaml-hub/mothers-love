# Detailed bubbles and focused new-recognition arrivals

## Reuse the visual progress without replacing the real app contract

The primary visual source is the **latest four-study atelier** in `prototypes/01_bubbles_latest/`. The three-study alternate in `prototypes/02_bubbles_alternative/` contributes additional silk/petal ideas and fitting examples. They use their own HTML/CSS/canvas/WebGL composition and sample clocks; neither is a drop-in production engine.

The current app owns depth-tested world-space recognition faces, rim/spark objects, hit targets, disposal, feed fingerprinting, selection and arrival residency. Preserve that ownership. Port the atelier's shell appearance, text hierarchy and exterior expression concept into the existing world-space group. Accessible HTML equivalents may remain available off the healthy 3D reading surface; a fixed card must not replace the bubble in the world.

## Bubble anatomy

Maintain a clearly circular silhouette at its reading poses. Preserve the recipient's entire allowed name, exact message, thank-you treatment, canonical expression identity, and public appreciation/reaction information only when the actual contract supplies it. Show a subtle page marker when the content continues. Never make important words part of an unreadably curved texture just to appear more 3D.

Use a smooth dome/film, thin layered rim, moving broad highlights, subdued iridescent edge variation, gentle internal color, and a quiet center. Detail should make the bubble feel carefully made, not distract from the words. Bloom, rim glints, foreground particles, cosmetic wires and scenery must not traverse the measured text safety region during the reading moment.

No real submitted-name/author field may be exposed to imitate the image's attribution. The existing root AGENTS states submitted name is admin-only. No generic "From your children" or "From friends" should be appended to a real record as fact. The concepts are visual references, not new public data definitions.

## Assigned expression satellites

Use only that record's distinct, valid expression IDs. Map to the current canonical artwork, phrase and color. Test zero, one and all nine. A symbol is not a bubble's internal chip anymore: it sits outside in a pearl medallion or precise camera-facing icon surface attached to the bubble's world parent.

Move the anchors through shallow 3D orbits with controlled speed, phase and distance. Calculate projected clearance for the entire medallion, its label where shown, the recognition's silhouette, and the text rectangle. Test the actual moving camera and the source record's maximum reading scale. Do not merely avoid a collision at one front screenshot.

In ordinary travel, depth and occlusion should remain coherent. During the featured reading shot, use a safe orbital corridor outside the projected reading disk. It is acceptable to adjust the path or suppress a tiny exterior label to preserve reading; it is not acceptable to put all expressions back inside the message or disable world depth globally.

The concept pictures use some invented stand-ins, while the recoverable prototypes include actual campaign icons. Keep the latter source identity. Improve medallion thickness/reflection or accurately traced relief only when the original symbol remains recognizable.

## Three entrance families

| Style | Meaningful sequence | Reading protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival Bloom | A light reaches a bud, layered petals open, the pearl rises, shell/name/message resolve, satellites establish orbit | Foreground petals spread away from the words before the required reading interval begins |
| Ribbon Portal | The viewer anticipates an opening, sheer veils turn and separate, the pearl approaches from depth, its content becomes prominent | Silk and structural gold must not form a cage over the text; depth order is deliberate |
| Constellation Gather | Distributed lights follow curved paths into the shell, a surface becomes visible, message and expressions are revealed | Particles retreat to shell/orbit/ambient roles while text is read; the gathering does not keep obscuring it |

Do not represent an old resident record's routine camera visit as a new publication. Keep source event identity and eligibility authoritative. Do not add audio because the images feel cinematic; the existing preview is visual unless a sound change is separately requested.

## State and queue integration

Adapt the visual phases to the existing `ArrivalDirector` states rather than inventing a second director that races it. Recognition eligibility -> queued -> approach/formation -> reveal -> complete reading -> settle -> resident/return. Safety cancellation can interrupt any phase immediately.

The same ID must appear once as an active arrival, then become resident after the handoff. It may not be duplicated in both pools during its entrance. A heart/reaction refresh is not a new arrival. Opening/reconnecting must not replay every resident record. A queued ID withdrawn by the public lifecycle must be removed before it is rendered.

Keep the complete reading interval ahead of another single arrival. Preserve the existing wave/group size and controls; new group aesthetics may share the same shell/effects, but no simultaneous unreadable wall of text. Do not treat the standalone queue capacities or auto-loop fixtures as production defaults.

## Clock and text invariants

Visual tempo, page timing, camera travel and public-data validity are separate concerns. The current settings/minimum and `reading.ts` define the required duration; each displayed page must complete. Text reveal/formation must not consume time when the full message is still invisible. Orbit speed does not change the message interval. Normal camera motion stays gentle enough for the content to remain legible; explicit reading hold freezes progression as currently intended.

Preserve every permitted source character, paragraph break, grapheme and name. Reuse or improve the current text-fitting logic with tests for combined characters, emoji, right-to-left text, long unbroken words and allowed maximum lengths. Do not impose an old prototype's arbitrary editor limit on the real submission API. Full-message accessible review remains available.

## Privacy and cleanup

The public snapshot remains the only data source for visible recognitions. Quiet/neutral/stale/expired/withdrawn states clear name, message, texture/canvas, hit-target ID, accessibility text, active/queued references and optional thumbnails immediately under current rules. Do not wait for a decorative exit. Never leave an old personal texture in a pool for the next record.

Stable unchanged records should not allocate new text textures every frame. Public content changes must invalidate the affected cached representation without resetting unrelated bubbles or extending validity. Effects and asset pools must remain bounded; no persistent personal archive, roster, popularity ordering or new location attribution is introduced.

## Required proof

Show at least one full entrance and complete reading interval for each style in the actual preview, plus a short/long/all-nine sample, a burst that queues, a reaction-only update that does not re-arrive, active and queued cancellation, and the existing camera-return behavior. Saved evidence must use fictional sample records and disclose any time compression in clips. Matching a standalone screenshot does not verify the integrated lifecycle.
