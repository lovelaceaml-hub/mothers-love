## A spatial world, not a playlist of unrelated canvases

Maintain one scene graph with real shared geography. Architectural kit pieces can repeat, but no two major landmarks should be distinguishable only by color. Use long-distance silhouettes to preview upcoming destinations. Keep roads/bridges/cloud passages anchored in the world, not screen-space transition tricks that contradict the camera's movement.

The original registry coordinates are retained in source. Do not move whole zones merely to improve a single capture without updating route connectors, arrival anchors, selection behavior and visibility/performance bounds. New decoration should have a named group and explicit zone ownership.

## Authored camera shot contract

For each destination, create an entry anchor, hero target, safe reading anchor/volume, foreground clearance and exit connector. A shot records its source/destination, position path, look-at path, field of view, minimum/maximum hold, interruption policy and fallback. Use eased velocity and near-zero roll. Look-at movement should be independently composed rather than always following the travel tangent.

Normal touring should remain hands-free. Current reading arcs can be made smaller or better aimed, but do not silently replace them with a frozen camera because a standalone bubble study uses one. Active reading must stay within its clear screen-space region throughout the arc. Preserve deliberate holds, owner rotation pause, reduced/still automatic cuts, and arrival return state.

## First high-quality connected passage

Begin low beside a garden bed, using a branch or petal as a foreground frame. Reveal a budding sample recognition without clipping through the flower. Reach its full reading pose and allow the existing reading policy to finish. Depart along a route that makes the sanctuary visible ahead. Reveal the heart through an opening of silk, then show a three-quarter beauty view. Continue toward the expression grove and feature one canonical expression. Rejoin the normal route without teleporting back to a stale saved camera position.

The artistic excerpt may be 60-90 seconds or longer when a message requires it. Do not shorten the recorded source reading or accelerate the application clock to hit a marketing-video duration. A separate condensed reel is fine only when explicitly described as condensed.

## Travel and occlusion rules

Every travel section needs something intentional to see, but not constant foreground obstruction. Reserve a clear camera collision corridor and reading forecourt. Avoid going through walls, inside trees, behind opaque terrain, or underneath a fully covering platform. Transparent silk can provide a controlled reveal; it must not become a constant veil over text.

Do not fix composition by globally disabling depth testing, placing the recognition forever on the camera, or hiding all geometry while it is read. Adjust layout, shot targeting, bubble anchor or local decorative clearance. The world and the bubble should still have coherent relative positions.

## Arrival interruption

New approved records are attention requests, not permission for camera whiplash. Complete or safely hand off the current reading interval, take a validated approach route, show formation and reveal, hold complete reading, settle the record once, then rejoin a compatible route node. Cancellation/quiet-state behavior may override aesthetics immediately. Group arrivals must preserve readable individual moments instead of forcing simultaneous text into one screenshot.
