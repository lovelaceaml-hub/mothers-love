## The change
1. Keep the contract: `createHeart()` returns `{canvas, draw(t)}` where `canvas` is now a real `<canvas>` element sized to the host (390 by 363 CSS px, backing store 780 by 726 for sharpness, `style="width:100%;height:100%"`), and `draw(t)` renders one frame for time t. mountHeart() keeps caching the renderer in the shared `heartRenderer` global and appending its canvas to `#heart-host`. Keep `alt` semantics by setting `aria-label="Sculpted rotating heart"` and `role="img"` on the canvas.
2. Build the mesh once at creation (top-level prefix rule: `PIP_HEART_*` for any top-level constants). Outline: the parametric heart curve x = 16 sin^3(u), y = 13 cos(u) - 5 cos(2u) - 2 cos(3u) - cos(4u) for u in 0..2 pi, N = 56 samples, normalised so the outline fits radius 1 with y up. Puff it into a solid: for M = 18 bands with phi from 0 to pi, vertex = (px * (0.22 + 0.78 sin phi), py * (0.22 + 0.78 sin phi), 0.62 cos phi). This gives about 1000 quads with a plump front and back and a rounded rim. Compute each quad's centroid and normal.
3. Per frame in draw(t): rotate the mesh by rotY = t * 0.9 (radians), rotX = 0.28 + 0.12 sin(t * 0.7) (a slow nod), a heartbeat scale of 1 + 0.035 * max(0, sin(t * 2.4))^6, project with a perspective camera (focal length 3.2 in mesh units, heart centred, scaled to fill about 88 percent of the canvas), cull quads whose rotated normal faces away from the camera, painter-sort the rest by depth, and fill each quad with Lambert plus Blinn specular lighting from a key light at upper left front and a soft fill from the right. Colors are the shipped heart palette only: base #e39baa, deep shadow #be6e85, highlight #fff0e9, rim #ffe8dc; blend between them by light intensity (no new hues). Draw a soft specular spot and a thin rim light so the volume reads from across the room. Draw a faint elliptical contact shadow under the heart in #c6748230 before the mesh (the host's drop-shadow filter stays).
4. Glints: 12 tiny sparks orbit the heart in three dimensions (seeded phases and radii around 1.15 to 1.35 mesh units), drawn in two passes, behind the mesh when their z is negative and in front when positive, in #fff0e9 and #ffe8dc, alpha up to 0.85, size 1.5 to 3 px.
5. Still mode: draw(t) still renders one static frame (the coordinator stops calling it), no requestAnimationFrame of your own, ever. Guard every draw with the canvas being connected.
6. Performance: no per-frame allocation of vertex arrays (reuse typed arrays), one path per quad, no shadows or blurs on the canvas context. Target under 4 ms per frame on a laptop.
7. Everything else in the scene stays as shipped (floating hearts, bubbles, depth layers, the peace burst).
