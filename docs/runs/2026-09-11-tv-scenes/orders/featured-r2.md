## The change
1. Rotation through all nine. Add top-level `let FEATURED_BAG = []` and `let FEATURED_LAST = 0`, and a function `featuredNextId()` that refills the bag with a shuffled copy of ids 1 to 9 when empty (Fisher-Yates with Math.random), skips an id equal to FEATURED_LAST when it is the first draw of a fresh bag, records FEATURED_LAST and returns the id. Store the id for the current pass on `V` (for example `V.featuredId`) at markup time so mount, tick and place can read it. featured() calls featuredNextId() instead of reading S.featured. A second expression inside the pass: add a `tickAge(dt)` descriptor hook; when `S.sceneAge` crosses 15 s once per mount (guard with a flag on V) call `featuredAdvance()`, which draws the next id, flips the panel (item 3) and re-targets the bubbles (item 2). Never read S.featured anywhere in this file.
2. Bubbles follow the expression. At markup, choose up to three records carrying the id with `choose(exclude, id)`; when fewer than three exist, fill the remaining slots with `choose(exclude)` (any record) and pass `expression: null` for those so core replaces them freely; show the "No current recognitions" line only when the pool is empty. In featuredAdvance(), for every non-large actor set `a.expression = newId` (core's replaceActor reads it) and stagger the wave so at most one bubble is exiting at a time: `a.retireAt = V.clock + i * 4.2` for i in actor order, which core will pick up one at a time (an exit lasts 3.8 s). Also add the eyebrow update: the eyebrow text becomes `Featured Recognition · Expression 0N / 09` for the new id.
3. The expression panel, high design. Rebuild the panel markup as layered depth inside a perspective wrapper (`<div class="feature-stage">` at the same box as today's panel, `perspective: 1200px`; the panel inside carries `transform-style: preserve-3d`). Layers, all scoped in featured.css to `#tv .scene[data-scene="4"]`:
   - Aurora: two blurred blobs (`.feature-aurora` children, radial gradients of `var(--tint)` and #fffbed, `filter: blur(28px)`) drifting slowly behind the text on 14 s and 19 s loops.
   - Idle sway: the whole panel gently sways (`rotateY` between -4 and 4 deg, `rotateX` between -2 and 2 deg, 12 s ease-in-out infinite). Bubbles are not inside this wrapper, so the circularity rule is untouched; the wrapper box stays inside x 0..520, y 55..617.
   - Medallion depth: the medallion floats in front (`transform: translateZ(70px)`) with a 5 s bob; around it two concentric rings (`.feature-ring` elements, conic-gradient borders in #fff, #b48b37 at alpha and the tint) rotate in opposite directions (18 s and 27 s); and a ring of the other eight expression icons (`.feature-orbit` with eight `<img class="orbit-icon">` from `icon(id)`, 26 px) orbits the medallion at radius about 88 px on a 40 s loop, each icon counter-rotating so it stays upright.
   - Text: the name keeps its shimmer; the phrase is split into word spans (`.phrase-word`) that rise and fade in with a 70 ms stagger on mount and on every flip; the principle fades in after the phrase.
   - Light sweep: a diagonal band of #fff at alpha 0.28 crosses the panel every 9 s (`.feature-sweep`, overflow hidden on the panel).
   - Counter: the eyebrow's "0N / 09" digits flip (a short rotateX flip on a `.feature-count` span) when the expression changes.
   - Flip on advance: featuredAdvance() adds class `flip-out` (rotateY to -90 deg over 450 ms ease-in); on `animationend` (or a 460 ms timeout) it swaps the panel content for the new expression (medallion, name, principle, phrase words, tint custom property, orbit icons), removes `flip-out`, adds `flip-in` (rotateY from 90 deg to 0 over 500 ms ease-out with a glow pulse of the panel shadow), and removes `flip-in` when done. Guard against a flip while a previous flip is running.
   - All of the above is off under `#tv.still` and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no animation runs, the panel renders flat with its first expression, the phrase words are fully visible.
   Keep `.feature-panel` colors, radius, border and shadows as shipped; new elements use only `var(--tint)`, #fff, #fffbed, #b48b37 and alpha variants.
4. Sizing: nothing overflows the scene root (scrollWidth and scrollHeight never exceed client size); every panel layer stays inside the panel box; the orbit icons never collide with the name.
