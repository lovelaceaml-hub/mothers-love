## The change
1. Lantern wave (CSS): a continuous ripple travels through the month. On `.day.past` and `.day.today` chain a second animation after the existing entry pulse: `calendarWave 12s ease-in-out infinite` with `animation-delay: calc(var(--day-index, 0) * 0.35s)` (keep the entry pulse's own delay by listing both animations with their own delays). The wave lifts the cell 3 px, brightens its background toward #fff and adds the gold glow already used (#D8B452 alpha), and scales the count `b` to 1.08 at the crest, for about 12 percent of the cycle. Compose with the existing pulse transforms so they do not fight (use the wave on the cell and the scale on `b`).
2. Today (CSS): `.day.today` gets a breathing ring (box-shadow of #D8B452 at alpha pulsing over 3 s) and a slow rotating conic halo on `::after` (inset -3 px, border-radius matching the cell, conic-gradient of transparent, #fff9 and #D8B45280, 14 s linear infinite, `pointer-events:none`).
3. Expression rows (CSS plus one line of JS): in mount set `--expr-index` on each `.calendar-expression` (0 to 8); a `calendarRowGlow 12.6s linear infinite` animation with `animation-delay: calc(var(--expr-index) * 1.4s)` lifts each row's background toward #fff and its `b` toward #b4677b brightness for 1.4 s in turn, so a highlight walks down the nine rows.
4. Sparkles and light sweep (canvas plus a `tick(dt)` hook): in mount append `<canvas class="calendar-fx" width="1812" height="636" aria-hidden="true">` to the scene root (CSS: absolute, inset 0, 1812 by 636, `pointer-events:none`, z-index above the panels but it must never intercept the day clicks, which the Studio preview uses). Each tick, when not still: every 0.35 s spawn a sparkle from a random `.day.past` or `.day.today` whose count `b` is above zero (read positions once per mount into a cached array of cell centres in stage coordinates: convert each cell's getBoundingClientRect relative to the scene root's rect and divide by the scene root's current scale, which is `sceneRoot.getBoundingClientRect().width / 1812`; recompute the cache when the selected day changes because renderNow rebuilds the scene).
