# Lane 0 critique: Plan 17, TV text fit and Plan 15 release

## 1. Lane 3's acceptance criterion contradicts its own work order (highest severity)

Lane 3's brief says: port production's Plan 12 scenes 8 and 9 into the split scene files. Its acceptance line says: "existing scene specs pass." Those two instructions cannot both be true.

`tests/color-polish-scenes/peacemakers.spec.ts` and `peace-in-practice.spec.ts` in the Plan 15 preview (worktree `mothers-love-tv17-critic`, branch `codex/tv15-full-preview`) test the Plan 15 redesign Anthony rejected, not the Plan 12 content he wants restored: `cardShape(frame, '.puzzle-card', 'puzzle')`, a `peacemakers_piece_hold`/`peacemakers_piece_joined` state machine, `#heart-cards .heart-card`, and a canvas hue check at `#heart-host canvas` (peace-in-practice.spec.ts line 117). Production's `renderer.js` at `b7d92a0` has none of these selectors or that puzzle-piece state machine (confirmed by grep across the file); its scene 8/9 markup is the older Plan 12 layout entirely. If Lane 3 does decision 9's actual job, these existing specs fail by construction, since they assert DOM shapes and a state machine the ported content will not have.

The plan does give Lane 3 ownership of "their scene specs," so it can rewrite them, but the acceptance text never says so. As written, a writer can satisfy "existing scene specs pass" only by leaving the Plan 15 puzzle/heart-card content in place, which is the opposite of decision 9. This is a plan defect, not a lane execution risk: fix the acceptance line before Lane 3 lands, or it will either stall on an impossible check or pass by quietly not doing the port.

## 2. Decision 9 ("show exactly what the live TV shows today") is unsatisfiable once the branch also carries Plan 15's transition system

Decision 9 and Lane 3's brief ask for parity with today's live TV. But "today's live TV" (`b7d92a0`) uses a single, fixed 1.15 s crossfade (`renderer.js` line 285-308: `transition={old,next,age:0,duration:1.15}`, linear translateX/scale/opacity tween). The Plan 15 core this plan builds on top of, `public/color-polish/core/transitions.js`, replaces that with a four-style system chosen from a shuffled bag (`TRANSITION_STYLE_IDS = ['flood','tide','vortex','storm']`, `TRANSITION_BAG`/`TRANSITION_BAG.shift()`). Once Lane 3's ported scenes 8/9 sit inside this stage, every time the rotation swings into or out of Peacemakers or Peace in Practice it will use flood, tide, vortex, or storm at random, never the crossfade Anthony saw and approved as "exactly what the live TV shows today." Lane 3's acceptance criterion only samples static frames at 0/6/14 s, so it cannot catch this: the scene's interior can match pixel-for-pixel while the surrounding transition experience is materially different from what was approved. The plan needs to say explicitly whether decision 9 covers the transition in/out or only the scene interior; right now that is a guess left to the lane and to whoever compares captures at Section Z.

## 3. Merging production `b7d92a0` into the preview branch has a real code conflict the plan does not name or assign

`git merge-tree 1532068 5a24f0b b7d92a0` (merge base 1532068, preview tip, production tip) shows a genuine, non-trivial conflict in `src/components/display/color-polish/renderer.tsx`: the preview side added `const PREVIEW_BUILD = process.env.COLOR_POLISH_PREVIEW_BUILD === 'true'` at the same line where production added `const QR_TARGET_URL = 'https://mothers-love.vercel.app/'` plus a rewritten `buildQrDataUrl` call site. Resolving this naively (accept either side, or a careless "keep both" paste) can silently drop either the preview/production sandbox-attribute gating (`sandbox={PREVIEW_BUILD ? ... : ...}`, `expectedOrigin`) or the September 18 QR-to-home-page fix that decision 11 explicitly requires ("neither is undone"). `docs/HANDOFF.md` also conflicts (two independent September sections colliding at the same insertion point) — mechanical, but still a manual resolution, and Section Z's brief ("merge production b7d92a0 ... confirm the QR payload decodes") does not mention this file or assign anyone to resolve it correctly rather than just "make it compile." Everything else in the diffstat (PublicFeedProvider.tsx, feed-types.ts, public-feed.ts, the three feed tests) merges cleanly because the preview branch never touched those files, so the risk is narrow but concrete and unaddressed.

## 4. The probe/spec methodology already failed to catch this exact class of defect once

Plan 15's own `welcome.spec.ts` and `orbit.spec.ts` already assert "no overflow" (`noOverflow` helper, scroll size vs. client size) and the project already captured probe screenshots before Anthony watched the TV. He still found bubble text running past the rim and boxes crossing the screen edge on the physical set. Plan 17 proposes the same kind of check — a `textFits`/bounding-box assertion in a headless Chromium viewport at 1920x1080 and 3840x2160 — as the acceptance gate, without stating what is different this time that will catch what the first round missed. Two candidate causes are visible in the repo and neither is addressed:
- The probe does wait for `document.fonts.ready` (`scripts/color-polish-renderer-probe.mjs` line 62) before capturing, but nothing in `core/host.js`/`boot.js` confirms the live runtime itself defers initial bubble sizing until fonts are ready; a flash of system-font sizing on a slow TV load could size a bubble before the approved font metrics apply, and the probe would never see it.
- The real church TV's effective viewport (device resolution, OS/browser scaling, overscan) is still unconfirmed. AGENTS.md itself flags "Physical-TV/phone commissioning is a distinct unexecuted check until Anthony supplies actual evidence." Testing at exactly 1920x1080 and 3840x2160 assumes the TV's browser renders at one of those two logical pixel sizes with no scaling; if the device runs at 125% OS scaling, a non-native resolution, or letterboxes into a smaller safe area, code that "fits" at the tested widths can still clip on the actual hardware, and Section Z's plan is a Vercel preview link Anthony views "on the TV" only after the rest of the checks already passed — there is no step that first confirms the TV's actual rendering geometry before lanes optimize against a guessed one.

## 5. "Mostly-long fixture" is undefined against the product's real 500-grapheme ceiling

The product invariant (AGENTS.md) allows messages up to 500 graphemes. Lane 1's acceptance criterion says the `textFits` assertion must pass "with the mostly-long fixture," with no defined length, no grapheme-vs-character distinction, and no requirement to include a message at or near the true 500-grapheme maximum. The existing fixture at `docs/design/color-polish-v6/fixtures.json` tops out around 90 characters; existing long-message tests elsewhere (`orbit.spec.ts` line 437-444, `.padEnd(210, '!')`) use ASCII character counts, not graphemes, and stop well short of 500. If Lane 1 builds "mostly-long" from what already exists in the repo, it will validate a fixture roughly a fifth of the real ceiling, and a genuinely maximal recognition message submitted by a real person could still overflow a bubble that the test suite calls fit. The plan should pin an exact fixture (character count and at least one true 500-grapheme, non-ASCII-safe case) rather than leaving "mostly-long" to be defined ad hoc by whichever writer builds it.

## 6. Lane worktree paths in the plan are already wrong, and nothing in the plan or Build log corrects them

PLAN-17 Section "Branch and worktree model" and each lane header give the worktree paths as `$HOME/Projects/mothers-love-worktrees/tv17-<lane>`. The actual lanes (per `docs/runs/2026-09-18-tv-fit/INDEX.md` and coordinator facts) run at `$HOME/.claude/worktrees/mothers-love-tv17-<fit|expressions|restore>`, a different filesystem root entirely, while Section Z's integration worktree is still the plan's original path. Section Z's own instructions ("from the tv17/pick worktree, copy `$HOME/Projects/mothers-love/.vercel` into it") and the KICKOFF verify commands are written against the plan's path model. Nothing in the plan file itself, its Build log, or INDEX.md's "Baselines" table records this substitution as a correction — it exists only as an out-of-band coordinator fact handed to this critique, which is precisely the kind of undocumented runtime change the shared policy requires be recorded at a section boundary before work resumes. If the real paths are not written back into the plan or Build log, later resumers (a fresh chat, Anthony, a reviewer re-running the verify commands) will follow the plan's stated paths and fail or silently touch the wrong worktree.

## 7. Every lane's actual writer contradicts the plan's routing table, undocumented

The plan assigns Lanes 1-3 to "gpt-5.6-luna via codex-dispatch... otherwise Gemini 3.8 Flash (High) via agy," reviewed by a separate "controller = Claude Sonnet 5." INDEX.md instead shows `model: sonnet-5` for every lane, L0 through L3, with `review: sonnet-5-high` for L1-L3 — the same model tier drafting and grading its own diff. The KICKOFF file explains the Gemini substitution (headless permission gate) but not the further substitution to Claude Sonnet 5 in place of both Luna and Gemini, and neither the plan's Build log nor INDEX.md's decisions log records this as an approved deviation. This is exactly the situation the cross-cutting rule "a lane's runtime changes only at a section boundary, recorded in the Build log... an ambiguity that would change the result gets one question to Anthony" is meant to prevent, and "writer never grades its own code" is weakened when the fallback writer and the fallback's own reviewer are the same model family with no independent check.

## What I checked and could not fault
The branch/worktree-per-lane file ownership (welcome/orbit/milestone vs. expressions vs. peacemakers/peace-in-practice) is genuinely disjoint with no overlapping files across Lanes 1-3. Decisions 1-8 and 10 (bubble growth, Orbit centre circle, Goal panel margins, Words-that-bring-peace inset math) are concrete and testable as stated, and Lane 2's 30-sample motion-envelope acceptance criterion is a reasonable, specific check. I did not find a rollback gap beyond what is already named (the plan does record a rollback target and gates production on Anthony's TV approval), and Featured/Garden/Status/Calendar are correctly left untouched and out of scope.
