## Part A: nothing is cut off at the stage's edges
Anthony on the church TV: on Featured the far left side is cut off, and this is common on every screen; the recognition area should run end to end. Evidence: /Users/anthony/Projects/mothers-love/docs/runs/2026-09-18-tv-fit/logs/captures/L3-after2/w1920-ms6000/featured.png shows the pink expression card's glow sliced by a hard vertical line at x=54. Cause: renderer.css `.stage { overflow:hidden }` (also `.scene` and `.event-stage`) on a stage inset 54 px from the screen edges.
Work: let scene content, glows and shadows paint out to the real screen edges left and right (and up to the header rule, down to the feed rail) instead of being sliced at the stage box. Do not move or resize scene content: every scene's layout coordinates stay exactly as they are (Anthony approved those scenes). The feed rail panel and header must never be painted over by ordinary scene content. The same capture also shows square notches on the pink card (about x 240 to 500, y 605 to 760); find out whether that is real or a headless artifact and report; fix only if it is inside your allowlist.

## Part B: the arrival ("entrance") is bright, heavenly and full screen
Anthony: he does not like how the entrance darkens the background; it should be bright and heavenly, and it should cover the whole screen, including over the live recognition scrolling, not only the recognition area.
Work: remove the near-black backdrop (scenes/arrival.css, rgba(14,18,17,0.9)). Reduced motion: static bright backdrop, no rays animation. The feed rail's own code and look are not changed.
