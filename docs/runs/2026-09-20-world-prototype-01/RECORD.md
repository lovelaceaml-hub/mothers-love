## Implementation

Thirteen resident locations in one Three.js renderer, pearl islands, flower beds, colored groves, heart and silk, globe, orbit, rising fountain, calendar terraces, pavilion, river, practice and promise sculptures. Locally authored sky/water shaders, cloud/particle textures, static geometry batching and adaptive pixel resolution. No purchased assets, external runtime fonts, or remote imagery.

The validated read-only public snapshot drives recognitions, insights and settings. A monotonic clock enforces expiry/staleness. Arrivals deduplicate after a baseline, queue in waves of five, provide full reading time, and restore the saved camera route. Full-list reading, expression filtering, calendar stories, held reading and message pages support observation at a comfortable pace.
