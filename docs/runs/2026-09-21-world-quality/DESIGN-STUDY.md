The study requires both `source=sample` and `art=design`. The ordinary cinematic and `art=classic` paths remain available. The sample mode selector is locked in the study. The first fictional sample has three canonical selections to make the exterior orbit easy to assess; live records are not altered.

The original sculpted heart is byte-preserved and receives a rose material, with independent silk surfaces around it. Flower petals and branches are procedural meshes. Compatible static batches preserve all geometry attributes, including UVs; imported heart, transparent surfaces and animated cloth stay outside them.
