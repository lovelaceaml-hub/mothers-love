# Anthony's correction: the transition is a vortex of REAL recognitions

Recorded 2026-09-12T07:30Z by the coordinator (Opus 5 high), from Anthony's message after watching the merged build: "transitions are still wrong. it should be like the vortex of recognitions on the goal scene. it should vortex with REAL recognition bubbles and covering the screen completely. when the screen is covered then the next scene will come but its still covered. now that is the point the vortex leaves the screen and now the scene is reavealed"

## What he is asking for, restated

1. The motion is the **vortex of recognitions from the goal scene**, that is scene 11's tribute stream: recognition pearls travelling a perspective helix, near ones large and sharp, far ones small and blurred.
2. The things that vortex are **real recognition bubbles**, carrying real recipients and their words, not decorative icon discs.
3. They **cover the screen completely**. No part of either scene visible.
4. **The scene changes while the cover is still complete.** The swap is hidden inside the covered moment, not at the leading edge of it.
5. **Then the vortex leaves**, and the new scene is revealed by its departure.

Points 3 to 5 are the phase machine that already exists; points 1 and 2 are what is actually wrong.

## This overrides two earlier rules, and he is entitled to

The plan's decision 5 said the flood's decorative bubbles "carry expression icons and colors, never text", and the coordinator turned that into a hard rule in every transition work order. Anthony is now asking for the opposite: the bubbles are the recognitions. His instruction wins over his own earlier decision.

The four-style flood family (flood, tide, vortex, storm) is also superseded as the *primary* look. The vortex is what he wants. Variety stays only as different flavours of that one vortex, not as four unrelated effects.

## Why the previous two attempts missed

The first attempt swept 24 decorative bubbles across a fully visible background, because the coordinator capped the count at 24 on a performance objection when the plan allowed 40. The second attempt fixed the coverage but did it with an opaque gradient wash, which satisfies "cannot see the background" while being exactly the wrong thing: a flat colour sweeping over the screen rather than a crowd of recognitions filling it. Both failures were the coordinator solving the letter of the requirement instead of the picture Anthony described.

## Geometry that guarantees full coverage

Circles of radius r on a grid of spacing sx by sy cover the plane when r is at least sqrt(sx squared + sy squared) / 2. On the 1812 by 636 stage an 8 by 6 grid gives sx 226.5 and sy 106, so r must be at least 125 and the bubble diameter at least 250. Forty-eight bubbles at 260 px therefore cover the stage completely by construction rather than by hoping enough of them overlap. The wash stays underneath as a guarantee of last resort, not as the visual.

## Constraint the coordinator must respect while doing this

A recognition bubble is circular and its text must not be truncated. Forty-eight real bubbles each measured with fitCircle is forty-eight layout passes at the start of every transition, which is a genuine hitch risk on the TV, so the frame time at 3840 is measured during the cover phase before this is called done, and the fallback if it is too slow is fewer, larger bubbles rather than fake ones.
