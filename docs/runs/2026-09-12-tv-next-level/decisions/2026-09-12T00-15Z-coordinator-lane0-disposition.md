**O2, the Peacemakers 40 second window. The critic's arithmetic is right and the gap report's fix is the one it asked for.

**O3, the probe cannot see transitions. The plan's line 120 additive probe change (log the transition style per scene change) is dropped, and Section B's acceptance moves wholly into its own spec. Section B therefore stops touching scripts/color-polish-renderer-probe.mjs at all, which also removes the last reason Section B and Section C had to coordinate.

## Accepted, should-fix

**O5, Section A's core/stage.js ownership says "onLeave only" but speech needs postRender and coordinator. Accepted as a plan-text correction.** Section A has already wired both, correctly: `postRender` mounts speech when the descriptor has speakers, and `coordinator` calls `tickSpeech(dt)` inside the existing non-still branch. **O6, the luminance check is not expressible in computed styles. Accepted, already fixed.** gaps/arrival.md G3 replaced it with two deterministic assertions: `#stage` still carries `.dimmed` (computed opacity at or below 0.23 and a blur in its filter) and the frame backdrop's computed background alpha at or above 0.88.

**O7, S.shownCount is never incremented by arrivals or the milestone stream. Accepted, and this one had no fix anywhere yet.** Section A's `markShown(id)` is called only from `addOrb`. `eventElement` in core/arrivals.js and `tributeContent` in scenes/milestone.js both put records on screen without going through `addOrb`, so records shown by an arrival or in the milestone stream would never be counted and `chooseRandom` would keep favouring them. **Resolution:** `markShown(r.id)` is added to Section C's work and acceptance (in `eventElement`) and to Section 9's (in `tributeContent`), both recorded in their gap reports. Section A's record file already notes that those two lanes own their own calls.

**O8, the Peace in Practice reading time and heart geometry were undefined. Accepted, already fixed.** gaps/peace-in-practice.md G3 sets the reading time to `Math.max(9, message.length/14)`, the same pace every other scene uses, and explicitly rejects decision 4's Peacemakers formula for this scene; G4 fixes the card's inner text rect at 62 percent of width and 48 percent of height, centred at 40 percent height, so the heart's taper is accounted for.

**O9, the phrase cannot be read on a 50 px coin, and putting it on both the coin and the card is redundant. Accepted, and the gap report was wrong.** gaps/status.md G1 as first written had the phrase on the coin's reverse face **and** as the card's primary label, which is exactly the contradiction the critic names. **Resolution:** the coin's reverse face carries the expression number and a small icon treatment only; the phrase lives in the card's text block as the primary label, with the expression name above it as the eyebrow. gaps/status.md G1 and its acceptance are rewritten accordingly. This is the critique catching a coordinator error, not a plan error.

**O10, CIRCULARITY_EXEMPT keys by slug while the DOM carries numeric scene ids. Accepted, already fixed.** orders/base-r1.md requires `circularity` to read `getState().scene` and map the renderer id back to a slug through the existing `SCENE_IDS` map before testing the exemption.

**O11, a 4K frame time on a Mac is no signal for the TV's GPU. The plan's Rollback section already names the same 24 as its first lever, so this simply starts there. The broader ask, a reduced-complexity device mode, is out of scope for this plan and is recorded as a follow-up rather than added.

**O14, an emergency revert leaves codex/color-polish-v6 holding the broken merge. Accepted.** **Resolution:** Section Z's rollback steps require the revert to be applied to **both** codex/mothers-love and codex/color-polish-v6, so no later lane cut from the development branch reintroduces a reverted defect. ## Rejected

Rejected on the evidence.** globe.png is 744 by 691 and shows **four figures** standing around the world: a girl in a green top with a raised hand, a boy in red stripes, a girl in a dark green dress, and a man in a light shirt lifting a puzzle piece out of the globe. The coordinator opened the image and measured all four heads; the anchors are in gaps/peacemakers.md G6 with their source and stage coordinates. The critic could not open the PNG (it read the assets directory listing only, as its own "What I checked" says) and inferred the absence of people from the filename. Anthony's brief asks for the bubbles on this scene in his own words, so removing them would also contradict his instruction. The plan's Assumptions line about "if it carries no figures the bubbles rise from three points on the world's rim" is now moot and is recorded as resolved.

**O13, "restore the milestone contributor pool filter." Rejected as written, accepted in part.** Anthony's brief says "all recognitions can be shown, not limited to recent ones", and decision 1 already bounds that to the seven-day pool. Restoring the week-and-goal filter would undo his explicit instruction, so the filter does not come back. The critic's underlying point is fair, though: milestone contributors should not be able to vanish from the milestone scene. **Resolution:** `contributorPool()` returns the whole eligible pool, but `mountTributes` seeds milestone contributors into the first nodes before the remaining nodes are drawn by `chooseRandom`, so contributors are guaranteed a place and everyone else can still appear. The gold rim keeps marking goal-supporting records. gaps/milestone.md G3 and G4 are updated.
