## Deviations and questions for the coordinator

1. The TV page is opt in (`?view=tv`), see above.
2. Heart counts: the public snapshot has no reaction counts and the data rule forbids another
   endpoint, so live bubbles show no number; sample bubbles show a fictional count.
3. Small bubbles, and the second big bubble (which stands back at about 420 px), show the name
   and the first three lines of the message with an ellipsis; the whole message shows when that
   record takes its turn in the held bubble (the second big one does on the next loop). The
   held and the arriving bubbles never cut: they scroll.
4. The hold rule sets its own floor, so the owner's reading seconds setting is not read on
   the TV page (the formula is one of the rulings Anthony may overrule).
5. The camera's reading arc is narrower on the TV page (`CameraDirector.arc`, defaults
   unchanged) so the held bubble keeps its reading size.
6. Everyday loop time is about 4.6 minutes; thirteen messages at the 20 s cap would take
   about 6.3 minutes.
