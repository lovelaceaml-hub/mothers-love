## Redesign after Anthony's review (2026-09-24)

After the all places commit (9ee0de9) Anthony wrote: "The recognitions aren't thoughtfully
placed or designed. They're just put randomly and cover everything. They aren't even connected
to the 3d world scenes. This needs to be fixed". Cause: the bubbles had been placed by screen
position from the tour camera and sized up to meet the text rule, ten per place, ignoring the
world's designed hero spots (WORLD-INTEGRATION 6.2, 6.5) that the scenery was built around.

Anthony's choices (asked with four options each): bubbles rest on things in the scene; one big
and three or four small in view; the camera flies close to read; redo the Garden first.

What changed (Garden only; `TV_BUBBLE_PLACES` is back to the Garden, and every other place keeps
its study pearls until his yes):
- `prototype/tv-anchors.ts`: every Garden bubble is anchored to an object in the district's own
  layout frame: the main bubble at the designed hero spot over the pond, held by a gold stem from
  the lotus; the second on a stem from the left rose bed; small ones hanging on a gold chain in
  front of the left arch, on the back right tree's crown (its real surface, from the canopy
  puffs), on the right bench's seat, on two lily pads and hanging from the right arch; two on
  the gold rails of every bridge out of the Garden. Natural sizes, no drift.
- The camera shows the whole place for 2 s, glides in to a reading pose where the main bubble
  is about 780 px tall and right of centre with the lotus in view, holds for the message, then
  pulls back (`tvReadingPose`, `tvFocusWeight`; the hold adds 7 s for the moves).
- Small bubbles are a glance: "Thank you," the name and the heart count at 22 px or more.
- The screen position layouts, the river runs and the panel layout are removed.
- Tests (`tests/world/tv-anchors.test.ts`): one big and three or four small in the tour view,
  150 px or more, clear of each other, of the main bubble's gold ring, of the title, the QR code,
  the live label and the ticker; the others off frame or wholly behind the main bubble; no
  bubble inside a tree crown it does not rest on (puff by puff); the main message 36 px or more
  in the reading pose from anywhere on the arc, with the lotus in the picture; the bridge
  bubbles on the rails of the 13 bridges the design world builds.
- Also fixed from the second review (on 9ee0de9): a tap no longer holds a bubble on the TV page;
  the TV page starts on Automatic quality; a place's visit is counted when the tour settles at
  the next place (its bubbles no longer swap while the camera leaves); bridge bubbles have
  in and out distances; the feature panel no longer spills over the ticker; no stray space in
  the reading panel's class when the TV page is off.

## Second redesign: whole messages that flow (2026-09-24)

Anthony on the anchored Garden (dd0db9d): "The bubbles need the actual recognition text
showing" and "Recognition bubbles can also naturally move and flow to show different
recognitions at the same scene". His choices: whole message, bigger bubbles (fewer fit); flow
through the scene; all 13 places now.

- Every place has two bubbles, both with the whole message: a main bubble resting in the gold
  calyx of a stem that rises from a lotus in an ivory basin, and a flowing bubble beside it.
- The flow (`prototype/tv-flow.ts`): as the camera arrives, the flowing bubble seen last time
  lifts off and floats up and away, fading, while a new one buds from its own lotus, rises and
  comes to rest with the place's next recognition. The main bubble changes the same way after
  the camera has left, so it never moves while it is read. At the Garden the flowing bubbles
  bud from the lit lotus on the pond; at the River from its lotus on the canal, and there two
  flow through each visit, floating on downstream.
- The spots (`prototype/tv-places.ts`) were chosen with a probe that measured candidate spheres
  against every mesh of the running scene (trees, arches, landmarks, kit pieces), then scored the
  tour view (the landmark left in view, the screen text clear). A second probe cast sight lines
  from the reading framing to 37 points on each bubble: at every place nothing stands between
  the camera and either bubble (the Grove's spot was lowered two units so its floating
  expression pieces pass above). The lotus basins, the stems, and every rise and float away
  path were probed too; the Pearl Pavilion's bubbles float out of its open front and the
  Grove's main bubble rises straight up. The Heart Sanctuary's bubbles float in front of its
  silk ribbons: a test sweeps the ribbons over ten minutes. The probe scripts are local tools,
  not part of the branch.
- A flowing bubble is sized to its words: the smallest diameter, in 10 px steps from 300 to
  380, at which its whole message fits at 23.5 px (`tvFitFlowDiameter`); a longer message
  scrolls while it rests.
- The camera shows the place for 1.5 s, glides in over 2.2 s to the reading framing (main bubble
  650 px on the right, flowing bubble up to 380 px beside it, the left third for the title, the
  panel and the share card), holds still there with a slow breath, then pulls back over 2.2 s.
  The glide now blends the finished tour pose (finesse included) toward the framing and is
  limited to a full glide in about 1.4 s, so an arrival or a changed record glides rather than jumps.
- Only the current place's bubbles show (and, while flying, the next place's), fading in and out,
  so no view carries another place's bubbles. The bridge bubbles are gone.
- The feature panels end above the share card, and the welcome panel leaves its duplicate
  "Share a recognition" code to the share card; the impact panel's three numbers share one row
  so its nine bars fit.
- Third review (on dd0db9d) addressed: narrow arc restored and the framing held still; no
  bridge bubbles in a place's views; the finesse no longer trails the glide; every TV bubble
  guards the glow layers behind its words; the glide is rate limited and a pause holds it; no
  per frame allocation in the reading pose or the pool; the camera's own field of view is used;
  a camera straight above the bubble reads from the district's front.

## Deviations and questions for the coordinator

1. The TV page is opt in (`?view=tv`), see above.
2. Heart counts: the public snapshot has no reaction counts and the data rule forbids another
   endpoint, so live bubbles show no number; sample bubbles show a fictional count.
3. One main and one flowing bubble per place, not four to eight small ones in view: whole
   messages need bigger bubbles, as Anthony chose. A place's small records take turns in its
   flowing bubble, one per arrival, so each still shows over the loops. A message over 130
   characters, more than a flowing bubble holds whole, shows in the main bubbles and the
   ticker only.
4. The hold rule sets its own floor, so the owner's reading seconds setting is not read on
   the TV page (the formula is one of the rulings Anthony may overrule).
5. The camera's reading arc is narrower on the TV page (`TV_ARC` through `CameraDirector.arc`,
   defaults unchanged), and in the reading framing the camera holds still with a slow breath,
   so the two bubbles keep their sizes and never drift together.
6. Everyday loop time is just under 6 minutes (359.8 s), the glide in and out included;
   twelve 200 character messages and one at the 20 s cap take about 6.5 (389.8 s).
7. The River's "two columns" are its main bubble and its stream of flowing bubbles.
8. Known and small, left as they are:
   - the Heart Sanctuary's tour view (the 1.5 s before the glide in) shows its main bubble's
     rim up to 33 px into the live badge and 20 px past the right edge (a test holds it there);
   - at the Pearl Pavilion the flowing bubble leaving as the camera arrives passes behind the
     title card for about 2 s while it fades;
   - the placeholder hosts can cross a bubble for a moment during a flight;
   - a narrow (portrait) screen frames the main bubble; where its flowing bubble lands is
     unchecked;
   - with the kit off (`kit=off`) the lotuses are absent;
   - a playlist of one scene never changes its bubbles, since they turn as the camera arrives
     and leaves;
   - each place in view draws about three more meshes for its lotus water, pads and petals;
   - sample arrivals follow the wall clock, so a renderer slower than a frame a second sees
     them back to back; at TV frame rates one per 90 s leaves the tour moving.
