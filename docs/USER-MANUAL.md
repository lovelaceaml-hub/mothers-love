# Using The Words of Mother's Love

## Read recognitions
Open the [home page](https://mothers-love.vercel.app) to see recent recognitions. New messages appear automatically. Scroll the recent recognitions box to read the latest 100, then choose Show older recognitions at the bottom to add 100 older ones at a time, back to the first recognition. Tap a heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. No sign-in is needed. Choose Share a recognition to open the form. At the bottom, Admin opens sign-in and TV View opens the public display directly. Use Pause animation to stop decorative movement.

## Share a recognition
Open the [recognition form](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/recognize). Enter your name and the recipient's name. Read the explanations beneath each expression, select the ones that fit, and write your message. Each expression has its own row. Selected expressions turn gold and show a checkmark. Preview it, then send it. Your submitted name is visible only to administrators.

Keep contact information and private details out of your message. If sending fails, keep the tab open and try again. A message received for review will appear only after approval.

The recognition page contains only the submission form. Return to the home page to read the latest 100 recognitions.

The [expression guide](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/expressions) explains all nine expressions with everyday examples.

## Sign in
Use your email and password at [admin sign-in](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/login). Your invitation email opens a page to set your password. If that link no longer works, request a new link from the sign-in page.

## Review and manage recognitions
Open the [review inbox](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/review) to check messages awaiting approval. Use [recognitions](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/recognitions) to search, edit or manage a message. Choose a reason when prompted.

The recognitions list shows every submission, oldest first, numbered in the order it came in, with the submitted name and the full message. Use Order to show the newest first; each submission keeps its number either way.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Publish | Makes an approved message available on the home page and TV |
| Hide | Removes the message from the home page and TV and keeps its contribution to totals |
| Invalidate | Removes a spam, duplicate or test message and its contribution to totals |
| Restore | Returns a message to review or display |
| Feature | Gives a message more attention on the TV |
| Permanent delete | Removes the message permanently; only the Owner can do this |

If another admin changes a message while you are editing, review their changes before saving again. [Insights](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/insights) shows campaign totals and lets you download a summary.

## Open TV View
Open [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) on the TV browser. No account or code is needed. Check the text from the viewing distance and scan the QR code with your phone. The TV shows eleven screens in turn, including Live Recognitions. To move to the next screen, click or tap the TV, or press the right arrow or the space bar.

For a screen that only shows the TV, open [TV View in kiosk mode](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display?kiosk=1) and press Enter full screen; the screen stays awake where the browser allows it. On an Android device, open TV View and use the browser's install option to add it as an app; the installed TV View opens full screen in landscape.

Use [Display controls](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display) to change motion, pause the display or stop showing messages. These settings apply to all open TV screens when they refresh. If an offline TV must stop showing a message immediately, turn off or blank that screen.

The Recognition Garden shows live recognitions as animated bubbles. New recognitions appear automatically, including while screen rotation is paused. Other rotating screens have a recent-recognition rail. Motion follows the display setting and the device's reduced-motion preference.

## Manage access
The Owner uses [Settings and access](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/settings) to invite moderators or remove account access. Moderators can manage recognitions and everyday display controls. Keep your password private.

Heart controls appear at the top right of recognition cards. Tap the heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. The number shows the confirmed count.

## Preview and arrange the TV program

Open [Display Studio](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display). Choose any of the eleven screen tabs and press Play or Replay. Safe sample is the default; Current approved explicitly loads public display content and clears it if verification fails or the content expires. Tempo, effects and timing preview your unsaved choices. Save display settings applies them to the TV on refresh.

On [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display), use Enable chimes on this screen once if the browser requires a sound gesture. Studio volume and event toggles still control sound. Pause also stops decorative motion. Long circle messages move to reveal their complete text, with dedicated reading pages as well. All nine Spotlight and Daily Practice expressions and all three One Kind Word lines rotate fairly. Home, school and work appear in sequence.

The latest owner choice keeps all selected effects active. The app does not automatically reduce effects. Actual TV speed, room audio and a real phone scan remain hardware checks, outside the selected software-only proof.

## The 3D world

The [3D world](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world) is its own page beside the TV View. It never replaces the [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) and is never part of the TV program: each screen shows one or the other, chosen by its address. To find it without typing, open [Display controls](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display) and choose **open the 3D world** at the bottom of the page.

The page opens the way a TV should show it: the camera tours thirteen places of kindness, recognitions float in the world as pearls, a ticker along the bottom lists the latest recognitions, and the QR code invites people to share one. It shows the same published recognitions and follows the same owner settings as the TV View: Quiet, Neutral, Pause arrivals, Pause rotation and Still all apply, and Quiet or Neutral clears every recognition from the world at once. With Still, or on a screen set to reduce motion, the ticker is hidden, because a ticker that cannot move would cut messages off at the edge of the screen; the pearls still show every word. Submitted names never appear.

Useful addresses:

| Address | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [/world](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world) | The TV tour with live recognitions |
| [/world?source=sample](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world?source=sample) | The same tour with fictional sample recognitions, labelled Sample recognitions and Fictional sample |
| [/world?view=explore](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world?view=explore) | The explorer: the world map, Read recognitions, Hold this recognition, Fullscreen and **Settings** |
| [/world?art=cinematic](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world?art=cinematic) or [/world?art=classic](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/world?art=classic) | The two earlier looks, kept as fallbacks |

**Picture quality** starts on Automatic, which steps the picture down if the screen cannot keep up. In the explorer, Settings also offers High and Low. The old preview address, `/display/world-preview`, still works and moves to `/world`.
