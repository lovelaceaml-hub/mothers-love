# Using The Words of Mother's Love

## Current release state
The full app is live and recognition sharing is open. The Owner can pause collection in display controls. The backend, certificate-verified restricted database connection and scheduled cleanup are active. Password setup is explicit and all administrators use email/password without an authenticator. Physical TV commissioning is separate.

## Explore the nine expressions
The [expression guide](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/expressions) contains all nine phrases, their descriptions and everyday examples. It remains available while recognition sharing is paused. The form's Why this matters buttons show the same examples without changing your selections.

## Share a recognition
Open the [recognition form](https://mothers-love.vercel.app), or scan the QR on the paired TV. Enter your first name and last initial for administrators, then the recipient's first name and last initial. Choose one or more of the nine expressions and write your appreciation, up to 500 characters as counted on screen. Preview shows what the campaign display may receive and leaves out your submitted name. Select Send recognition.

A ready receipt means the recognition is eligible for the campaign display. A review receipt means it was received privately for a moderator. Neither receipt proves a TV was on or someone saw it. If the response cannot be confirmed, keep this tab open and retry with the same entries. Recognize Someone Else starts an empty form. There are no member accounts or permanent saved drafts.

Keep health details, contact information and private disagreements out of the message. A name is self-reported. Message wording can still reveal the writer. See the [privacy page](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/privacy).

## Sign in and manage the campaign
Use [admin sign-in](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/login). Only invited campaign accounts have access. Owners and Moderators sign in with their individual email and password. No authenticator is required. Invitation and recovery links open [Set your password](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/set-password). A valid email-link session is required; expired links offer a fresh-link request. Never share an Owner password or a TV pairing code in a public message.

The [overview](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin) shows review work, totals, cleanup health and paired-browser status. Use the [review inbox](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/review) for held recognitions and the [recognition manager](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/recognitions) to search and filter active content. Open a recognition to edit its submitted name, recipient, expressions or message. Choose a reason category for substantive changes. If another admin has changed it, reload the current record before deciding what to change.

## Hide, invalidate, restore and delete
| Action | Use | Effect on totals |
|---|---|---|
| Hide | Valid recognition should leave the TV, including a recipient request | Keeps its valid contribution |
| Invalidate | Spam, duplicate or test entry was never a valid contribution | Reverses its contribution once |
| Restore | Return an eligible hidden or invalid record before expiry, with a reason | Hidden stays counted; valid reinstatement adds once |
| Permanent delete | Owner removes the active personal record | Valid contribution remains anonymous; invalid or never-published content adds nothing |

Feature selects a recognition for display emphasis. It never changes counts or extends its lifetime. All personal recognition content expires 168 hours after submission, including held or hidden time. Edits and restores keep the original deadline. After expiry there is no per-message adjustment or archive. Anonymous daily totals remain.

The [insights page](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/insights) reports recognitions and expression selections, not unique people or verified acts. A first valid publication fixes the Los Angeles reporting day. Weeks start Sunday. Later edits affect that original period. CSV contains aggregates only.

## Pair and operate a TV
The Owner opens [settings and access](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/settings) to create a one-time pairing code. On the TV browser open the [display](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) and enter the code. Codes expire after 10 minutes. A paired browser session lasts up to 30 days unless revoked. The code is not an administrator login.

Use [display controls](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display) for normal, Quiet or emergency neutral mode, Calm/Standard/Lively/Still motion, and pause controls. Quiet waits for manual release. Still suppresses movement. Emergency neutral removes recognition content. Safety removals and expiry continue even when rotation or arrivals are paused.

Saved means the server accepted a setting. Pending means the paired browser has not acknowledged it. Applied means a browser acknowledged that version; it does not prove the physical TV is on. If an offline TV must immediately stop showing a message, the on-site operator should turn off or blank that screen. The browser clears personal content after 60 seconds without a fresh verified snapshot, or at a recognition's earlier expiry.

## Account and recovery responsibilities
The Owner invites and revokes moderators and devices. The last active Owner cannot be disabled through the app. Revoked campaign membership is checked on subsequent operations. A campaign account does not grant access to other apps sharing the database. If the Owner loses access, the account holder must complete the provider's identity recovery before campaign access is restored. Do not create a bypass account or change another person's password to work around recovery.

Each church session needs a designated moderator, with Owner fallback. Before opening collection, confirm the review inbox, cleanup status and display pairing work. Collection automatically pauses if cleanup has not run within two minutes or personal records exceed the cleanup lag. It can resume after cleanup recovers while the stored Owner preference is open. Physical TV distance readability and a phone QR scan need an on-site check; automated browser evidence cannot certify them.
