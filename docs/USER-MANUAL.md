# Using The Words of Mother's Love

## Read recognitions
Open the [home page](https://mothers-love.vercel.app) to see recent recognitions. New messages appear automatically. Scroll the recent recognitions box to read the latest 100. Tap a heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. No sign-in is needed. Choose Share a recognition to open the form. At the bottom, Admin opens sign-in and TV View opens the public display directly. Use Pause animation to stop decorative movement.

## Share a recognition
Open the [recognition form](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/recognize). Enter your name and the recipient's name. Read the explanations beneath each expression, select the ones that fit, and write your message. Each expression has its own row. Selected expressions turn gold and show a checkmark. Preview it, then send it. Your submitted name is visible only to administrators.

Keep contact information and private details out of your message. If sending fails, keep the tab open and try again. A message received for review will appear only after approval.

The recognition page contains only the submission form. Return to the home page to read the latest 100 recognitions.

The [expression guide](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/expressions) explains all nine expressions with everyday examples.

## Sign in
Use your email and password at [admin sign-in](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/login). Your invitation email opens a page to set your password. If that link no longer works, request a new link from the sign-in page.

## Review and manage recognitions
Open the [review inbox](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/review) to check messages awaiting approval. Use [recognitions](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/recognitions) to search, edit or manage a message. Choose a reason when prompted.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Publish | Makes an approved message available on the home page and TV |
| Hide | Removes the message from the home page and TV and keeps its contribution to totals |
| Invalidate | Removes a spam, duplicate or test message and its contribution to totals |
| Restore | Returns a message to review or display |
| Feature | Gives a message more attention on the TV |
| Permanent delete | Removes the message permanently; only the Owner can do this |

If another admin changes a message while you are editing, review their changes before saving again. [Insights](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/insights) shows campaign totals and lets you download a summary.

## Open TV View
Open [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) on the TV browser. No account or code is needed. Check the text from the viewing distance and scan the QR code with your phone.

Use [Display controls](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display) to change motion, pause the display or stop showing messages. These settings apply to all open TV screens when they refresh. If an offline TV must stop showing a message immediately, turn off or blank that screen.

The Recognition Garden shows live recognitions as animated bubbles. New recognitions appear automatically, including while screen rotation is paused. Other rotating screens have a recent-recognition rail. Motion follows the display setting and the device's reduced-motion preference.

## Manage access
The Owner uses [Settings and access](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/settings) to invite moderators or remove account access. Moderators can manage recognitions and everyday display controls. Keep your password private.

Heart controls appear at the top right of recognition cards. Tap the heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. The number shows the confirmed count.

## Preview and arrange the TV program

Open [Display Studio](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display). Choose any of the ten screen tabs and press Play or Replay. Safe sample is the default; Current approved explicitly loads public display content and clears it if verification fails or the content expires. Tempo, effects and timing preview your unsaved choices. Save display settings applies them to the TV on refresh.

On [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display), use Enable chimes on this screen once if the browser requires a sound gesture. Studio volume and event toggles still control sound. Pause also stops decorative motion. Long circle messages move to reveal their complete text, with dedicated reading pages as well. All nine Spotlight and Daily Practice expressions and all three One Kind Word lines rotate fairly. Home, school and work appear in sequence.

The latest owner choice keeps all selected effects active. The app does not automatically reduce effects. Actual TV speed, room audio and a real phone scan remain hardware checks, outside the selected software-only proof.


## Heavenly World preview

The separate [world preview](https://mothers-love-ipk16tg3a-lvzion.vercel.app/display/world-preview) opens with **Live recognitions** from the campaign. For fictional demonstrations, choose Sample world in Settings or add `?source=sample` to the preview address. **World map** opens all thirteen locations. Garden, Sanctuary, and Expressions also have quick buttons. The tour starts automatically without a click. The camera flies in a slow arc among readable pearl bubbles above the gardens and along the river, then travels to the next location. These bubbles live inside the world: their position, size and depth change naturally as the camera passes them. They remain part of the scenery during travel.

**Read recognitions** opens complete messages and an expression filter. **Linger with this recognition** returns to its reading view and holds it. **Continue reading tour** resumes. Names and messages are drawn directly on the world bubbles. Long messages turn through numbered pages automatically; the full unbroken message is always available in Read recognitions. Previous and next arrows select another story. Clicking a bubble in the scenery selects and holds it. Use Continue reading tour to release it. In Garden of Days, choose a date to see its counts and currently available stories.

Animated fictional neighbors greet, gesture, smile and exchange hearts throughout the world. Their speech illustrates the campaign's words of kindness; the recognition bubbles show actual approved campaign messages in Live recognitions and fictional examples in Sample world. Soft sky ribbons, water, silk and light animate around the reading stops.

**Settings** controls speed, picture quality, and still view. In Sample world, **Preview grand entrance** demonstrates one fictional arrival rising inside the luminous world portal; **Preview five arrivals** demonstrates a wave with separate reading time for every person. Resume the tour and release a held recognition before starting a demonstration. Reduced motion gives a stable reading celebration and automatically changes locations with immediate cuts. Still view also continues the automatic tour; Pause tour or Hold this recognition intentionally stops it. The owner's arrival-pause setting suppresses live entrances.

Live recognitions is the default and uses the existing public TV feed and the owner's mode, motion, reading time, arrival pause, rotation pause, and scene selections. World stops have a 20-second minimum, extended until the complete message has received its reading time. They do not inherit the original TV playlist's three-minute Garden wait. A new verified publication enters after the current reading interval. Existing records are not replayed as new arrivals when opening or reconnecting. Quiet and unavailable states clear messages. The world does not submit recognitions or change settings.

**Hide details** clears location labels and controls while the bubbles remain in the environment; Show details or Escape brings the controls back. **Fullscreen** uses the browser's fullscreen support. If 3D cannot run, a still view retains the reading browser and all locations; Try 3D again recreates the renderer. The original [TV display](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) continues separately.

Vercel protects preview deployments. Use the final handoff's temporary share link or a signed-in owning Vercel session. Share links expire, without changing the original TV address. Sound is not part of this preview; celebrations are visual.
