# Using The Words of Mother's Love

## Read recognitions
Open the [home page](https://mothers-love.vercel.app) to see recent recognitions. New messages appear automatically. Scroll the recent recognitions box to read the latest 30. Tap a heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. No sign-in is needed. Choose Share a recognition to open the form. At the bottom, Admin opens sign-in and TV View opens the public display directly. Use Pause animation to stop decorative movement.

## Share a recognition
Open the [recognition form](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/recognize). Enter your first name and last initial, then the recipient's. Read the explanations beneath each expression, select the ones that fit, and write your message. Each expression has its own row. Selected expressions turn gold and show a checkmark. Preview it, then send it. Your submitted name is visible only to administrators.

Keep contact information and private details out of your message. If sending fails, keep the tab open and try again. A message received for review will appear only after approval.

A compact live feed appears below the form title, above the fields. Scroll it to read the latest 30 recognitions. Other public pages also show the live feed.

The [expression guide](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/expressions) explains all nine expressions with everyday examples.

## Sign in
Use your email and password at [admin sign-in](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/login). Your invitation email opens a page to set your password. If that link no longer works, request a new link from the sign-in page.

## Review and manage recognitions
Open the [review inbox](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/review) to check messages awaiting approval. Use [recognitions](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/recognitions) to search, edit or manage a message. Choose a reason when prompted.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Publish | Makes an approved message available on the home page and TV |
| Hide | Removes the message from the home page and TV and keeps its contribution to totals |
| Invalidate | Removes a spam, duplicate or test message and its contribution to totals |
| Restore | Returns a message to review or display |
| Feature | Gives a message more attention on the TV |
| Permanent delete | Removes the message permanently; only the Owner can do this |

If another admin changes a message while you are editing, review their changes before saving again. [Insights](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/insights) shows campaign totals and lets you download a summary.

## Open TV View
Open [TV View](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/display) on the TV browser. No account or code is needed. Check the text from the viewing distance and scan the QR code with your phone.

Use [Display controls](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/display) to change motion, pause the display or stop showing messages. These settings apply to all open TV screens when they refresh. If an offline TV must stop showing a message immediately, turn off or blank that screen.

The Recognition Garden shows live recognitions as animated bubbles. Other rotating screens have a recent-recognition rail. Motion follows the display setting and the device's reduced-motion preference.

## Manage access
The Owner uses [Settings and access](https://mothers-love.vercel.app/admin/settings) to invite moderators or remove account access. Moderators can manage recognitions and everyday display controls. Keep your password private.

Heart controls appear at the top right of recognition cards. Tap the heart to like a recognition, or tap it again to remove your like. The number shows the confirmed count.
