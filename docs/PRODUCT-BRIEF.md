# Product brief

## Outcome
One campaign helps members acknowledge expressions of Mother's Love and displays eligible appreciations in the approved warm Bubble Board. Exact title, tagline, all nine phrases and explanatory principles remain canonical. Public location branding and church logos are absent.

## People and first success
A visitor scans a QR, completes one short form and receives an honest receipt. A moderator reviews a held message or removes sensitive content without losing valid totals. The Owner secures individual admin access and opens the public TV link. The TV calmly presents recognitions, full campaign expressions and monthly aggregate progress.

## Approved scope
Public form and privacy page; secured Owner/Moderator workspace; one direct public display journey; short personal lifetime; atomic aggregate corrections; review holds; organic motion and static alternatives; sequential arrivals; complete expressions; aggregate reporting; privacy-safe backup and recovery controls.

## Deliberate exclusions
Member accounts for submission, public message browsing, directory lookup, raw exports, rankings, unique-member statistics, runtime AI, sound, permanent personal archives and unrelated app changes.

## Authority
The handoff approval register is preserved as history. Conversation decisions and CONTRACT.md finalize admin and data behavior. The later explicit request authorizes implementation and deployment; the existing database and Vercel campaign-only credential transfer are separately authorized. Technical defaults selected under delegated judgment are labeled in CONTRACT.md. No new paid resources were approved.
